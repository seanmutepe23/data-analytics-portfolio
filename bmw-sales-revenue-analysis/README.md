# BMW Sales & Revenue Analysis

## Overview

This project analyses BMW sales and revenue performance across different models, regions, fuel types and transmission types using Google Looker Studio.

## Objectives

The analysis examines:

- Sales performance over time
- Sales by BMW model
- Sales by fuel type
- Sales by region
- Sales by transmission type
- Revenue trends
- Revenue by region
- Revenue by model
- Revenue by transmission type

## Key Findings

The analysis identified differences in sales and revenue performance across BMW models and regions, including strong performance from models such as the X1 and differences in regional preferences for fuel and transmission types.

## Recommendations

The analysis recommends:

- Prioritising SUV production
- Expanding electric vehicle marketing in North and South America
- Targeting Asia with petrol and hybrid models
- Investing in manual transmission options in markets where demand is strongest
- Using diversified regional strategies to support growth

Google Looker Studio
